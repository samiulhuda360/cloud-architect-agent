# Cedar & Lark Credit Union — Technology Data Standard

## Purpose
Members trust us with their savings and their personal details. This standard tells project teams what the
technology team expects from any new system before it goes live.

## Location of member information
Member information is to be stored in Canada only, including copies held for disaster recovery and testing.

## Platform choices
To keep support manageable, projects only use the following approved Azure services:
1. App Service
2. Azure SQL Database
3. Blob Storage
4. Key Vault
5. Log Analytics
6. Application Gateway

Anything else needs a written exception from the Director of Technology.

## Spending
A project's cloud bill is capped at C$4,000 per month. Finance reviews the bill at month end.

## Backup retention
Database backups are required to be held for 60 days. A restore is tested before each major release.

## Training
New staff complete privacy training in their first month.
