# Penhallow Veterinary Group: IT Security Baseline

Issued by the Operations Director for all 23 practices. Review date: April 2027.

## Purpose

Pet owners give us their names, addresses, phone numbers and payment details, and our clinical notes describe their
animals' care. This baseline is the minimum every system and supplier must meet.

## Where we host

Our systems and the data in them are to be hosted in UK data centres.

## Databases

Every database must use a private link connection; public network access must be switched off. Keep database
backups for no less than 28 days.

## Keys

Encryption keys for clinical records must be generated and controlled by Penhallow, not by the cloud provider.

## Audit

Audit trails are kept for 2 years.

## Money

Hosting costs must remain below £1,800 a month across all practices.

## Staff and suppliers

Staff must lock their screens when they leave the consulting room. Suppliers must complete our security
questionnaire before they receive any data.
