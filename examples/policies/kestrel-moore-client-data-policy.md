# Kestrel & Moore Accountants: Client Data Handling Policy

Prepared by the Practice Manager. Reviewed by the partners in July 2026. This policy explains how we look after
the personal and financial information our clients trust us with, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 and the
Australian Privacy Principles.

## What this covers

Tax returns, financial statements, payroll files, identity documents and the emails that come with them, whether
they sit in our practice software, in file storage or in the bookkeeping platform we connect to.

## Where client data lives

Client records must remain in Australia. If a provider cannot say where it stores our data, we do not use it.

## Who can see it

Staff see the files for the clients they work on. No client data store may be accessible from the public internet;
staff connect through the firm's sign-in and the office network.

## Keeping and destroying records

Client files are kept for seven years after an engagement ends and are then securely deleted.

## Backups

Backups must be retained for 30 days, and one restore is tested each month.

## Costs

The monthly IT hosting cost must stay under A$2,500 including all environments.

## Breaches

Any suspected data breach is reported to the Practice Manager on the same day so that we can assess whether it is
an eligible data breach under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
